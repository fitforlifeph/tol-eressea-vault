We all have those little things that make us happy. They might be tiny, but they are some of the simplest joys of life and make our day a little bit better.

People are often too busy to appreciate the simple joys in life. We’re so focused on work, errands, and other responsibilities that we forget to take a step back and enjoy the moment. But the truth is, life is full of small moments of happiness if we only take the time to look for them.

No matter what is happening in our lives, we can always find something to appreciate and be grateful for.

## 101 Simple Joys in Life

1) Seeing the sunrise over the horizon – sunrise is always beautiful no matter where you are or what time of year it is.

2) The smell of newly cut grass – the fresh scent of grass after cutting the lawn always brings a smile to my face – and sometimes a sneeze too – but it reminds me that summer is right around the corner.

3) A cold breeze on a hot day – laying out on the grass on the hottest day of the year and feeling a cool breeze is pure bliss.

4) Suddenly remembering a word that you’ve been trying to recall – it’s hard not to smile when you remember the name of that actor or singer (without the help of Google).

5) The sound of rain falling on your roof during a storm – this is relaxing, especially if you’re snuggled up in bed or on the couch by the fireplace. Equally therapeutic is hearing raindrops fall down onto the fireplace!

6) Getting through a challenging workout – knowing that all those hours at the gym were worth it when you finish an effective workout session is always rewarding.

7) Falling asleep before 10pm and not waking until you’re refreshed and well rested – sometimes sleep wins out over staying up late watching TV or finishing off work projects.

8) Discovering a band you had no idea existed but loving their sound – finding a new artist to listen to is always exciting.

9) Fixing something that was broken – taking an object that doesn’t work and returning it to its original state is extremely satisfying.

10) Finally crossing an item off your to do list – once we complete a task, it’s always nice to check the “done” box and move on with our day.

*Read also: [How I Use a Monthly Checklist to Organise My Life](https://theplainsimplelife.com/monthly-checklist/)*

11) Being able to help someone in need – knowing you were able to offer assistance during a difficult time brings inner satisfaction.

12) Laughing until your stomach hurts – sometimes nothing else can make us smile like a good laugh! It’s such a simple pleasure, but one of the best ones around.

13) Starting the day with a brisk walk or jog – get your blood pumping, enjoy nature, and get rid of any stress before you head to work.

14) Eating breakfast outside during the summer – there’s nothing quite like a breakfast picnic in the warm weather to start your morning off.

*Read also: [Minimalist Morning Routine to Embrace Simple Living](https://theplainsimplelife.com/minimalist-morning-routine/)*

15) Seeing an amazing lightning storm over the horizon – lightning storms are often overlooked and underrated. They’re truly breathtaking. I often stay up until the early hours just to watch a lightning storm from my balcony.

16) Watching raindrops trickle down your window – rain droplets can be mesmerising, especially when viewed from inside.

17) Downright relishing in whatever food you’re craving at that moment – when you have a craving, it’s often the only thing that you can think about. The best part is getting to enjoy that food in its purest form.

18) Hot showers after getting caught in the rain – nothing beats a hot shower after getting caught in the storm. It’s rejuvenation and relaxation at its best.

19) Walking barefoot on grass or sand – feeling the soft texture of grass or the warmth of sand beneath your feet is one of life’s simple joys that many of us take for granted.

20) Warm blankets in wintertime – nothing feels quite like crawling into your warm bed with your cuddly blanket on cold, snowy days. If I could, I’d be buried under my covers 24/7 some days!

21) Receiving letters through snail mail – sending mail and receiving mail is something that we don’t do enough of these days. Receiving a hand-written letter in the mail is such a rarity and it’s always special.

22) The smell of fresh laundry – seeing clean clothes hanging up or folded neatly in drawers is satisfying, but nothing beats that “new” smell after taking them out of the washing machine.

*Read also: [13 Ways to Regain Control When Overwhelmed by Laundry](https://theplainsimplelife.com/overwhelmed-by-laundry/)*

23) Looking at old photos and laughing/crying about memories – reminiscing about previous times brings me great joy. I love going through old photo albums with friends and family.

A good life is a collection of happy memories

Denis Waitley

24) Holding a newborn baby for the first time – this never gets old. You may be an experienced parent now, but holding a newborn for the first time still gives that same feeling as it did the first time around.

25) Watching your favorite show or movie for the second/third/fourth/fifth time – watching movies and TV shows are great sources of entertainment, but there are some movies that are just so good that you have no choice but to watch them multiple times.

26) Going on a spontaneous adventure with friends – never be afraid of saying yes when your friends ask for your company on their adventures. Finding something interesting and adventurous to do is one of life’s many pleasures.

27) Hugging someone tightly because it feels so good – not all hugs are created equal. There are some hugs that simply feel extra special and comfortable. You know the very ones.

28) Taking a bath with relaxing essential oils – a warm bath makes any tense day much better, and essential oils also have therapeutic benefits that can be great for your overall health.

29) Cuddling with your pet – pets are amazing and cuddling with them is even better! They’re intuitive and when you may not be feeling the best, they can bring so much joy.

30) Getting an unexpected smile from a stranger – this is one of my favourite simple joys in life. Walking down the road and a stranger smiling at you. You will find that you then smile at someone else. It’s contageous! And the smiles keep getting passed along.

31) Meeting old school friends after years apart – catching up on what’s going on in each other’s lives is great, but there’s something extra special about seeing your old friends again after years apart. It’s like no time has passed at all!

32) Handwritten messages – seeing the time, effort and thought put into a handwritten message touches my heart. Handwriting is something so special that will never disappear.

33) Sharing good news with people who are genuinely happy for you – it never gets old seeing your family, friends, or even complete strangers light up when they hear good things happening in your life. Excitement is contagious!

34) Memories that warm your heart – whenever I think back on memories that fill my heart with warmth, I’m reminded what really matters, the little things that make us smile every day.

35) Drinking a cold glass of water when thirsty – there are few things more satisfying than drinking a cold glass of ice-cold water when you’re dehydrated.

36) Hitting the bed after a really long tiring day – it feels amazing crawling under the covers of my bed at night after an exhausting day. I love cozying up in my bed and getting some shut-eye.

37) Stealing a few more minutes to sleep – sometimes, there’s no better feeling than hitting snooze on your alarm clock for just a little longer and squeezing out every last bit of sleep that you can.

38) Realising how far you’ve come since the beginning – sometimes I look back on where I started and think about how much I’ve grown throughout my journey so far. Realising how far you’ve come makes you appreciate how far you can go

39) Finally getting rid of clutter that has been occupying unnecessary space in your home – clearing out the clutter is one of the best feelings in the world. Getting rid of unnecessary things that occupy your space, whether it be actual physical items or mental baggage, feels great and reduces stress and anxiety!

## Final thoughts on living in the moment and joying the simple joys of life

Appreciating the simple joys of life is something we should all make an effort to do more often because sometimes these little things are the only things that have the ability to brighten up our day.

When you stop taking life for granted and start noticing everything around you it’s amazing how much happiness starts to find its way into your everyday life! There are so many simple joys of living that bring us great joy every day – even if it’s just a quick coffee in the morning with friends before work or seeing the first daffodil pop out after winter.

But what’s important is being aware of these moments and developing gratitude for them. Because at the end of the day, once it’s all said and done, making sure we appreciate these small things can give us hope.
