A nine-minute New York Times video, not a written article. The Metropolitan Museum of Art acquired *Bélizaire and the Frey Children*, an 1837 Louisiana portrait in which the figure of an enslaved child had been painted over and left hidden for more than a century. The video tells the story of the painting's restoration and of Bélizaire himself.
